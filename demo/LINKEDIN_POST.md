Campus complaints don't fail because nobody cares. They fail because the campus forgets.

A geyser trips in Hostel A. It gets fixed. Next winter the same fault hits Hostel B, and the new caretaker starts from zero.

At hackCBS 9.0 we built **Sunwai** (सुनवाई, "a hearing"), a campus grievance swarm on top of Meko.

How it works:
- A student writes a complaint in their own words. A Desk agent recalls what the campus already knows, merges duplicates and answers with status, ETA and the reason for the ETA.
- Departments leave each other notes. When electricity staff record a power cut, the Hostel agent's datapack gets it, so the ETA is honest.
- A fix becomes campus knowledge only after the students it affected confirm it held and a warden approves it. Only then is it promoted to canon. The dean reads canon only.
- The swarm view shows every person, every agent and every Meko call as it happens. Nothing on that screen is drawn unless a call was logged.

Switch Meko off and the same flow still runs, but the later case starts from zero. The swarm view goes dark. That's the point.

Built by Team Syzygy (Atharv, Arun and me) with Meko for shared memory and an open model running on our own laptop, for the YugabyteDB "AI Agent Innovation powered by Meko" challenge.

Repo: https://github.com/Atharv-tw/sunwai
